The Incentive Trap • Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond (n = 5,179) [5]

Why Employers & Waterloo’s SPE Won’t Catch It

AI boosts novice output by 34% [5]. Because Waterloo’s Student Performance Evaluation (SPE) [8] rates job output, a student can get an outstanding evaluation without building engineering judgment.

What Employers See on the Job [4], [5]

Immediate Output Surges

Novice Worker Productivity Gain with AI [5] +34%
All Workers Average Productivity Gain [5] +14%
Initial Task Score Gain with Copilot [4] ≈ +14 pts
My Summer Backend Co-op: “AI was capable of doing everything I did on the job, which hindered my learning experience since my employer preferred using AI as it produced working code much faster.”
What the End-of-Term SPE Misses [3], [4], [8]

Hidden Skill & Safety Deficit

1ST & 2ND CO-OP TERMS
Lose the Most

Students with strong fundamentals work faster, while novices still learning lose foundational debugging skills.

PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY
Safety Risk

Engineers are responsible for work they approve. A graduate who cannot debug AI code is a safety risk.

Waterloo’s Leverage: Over 8,000 employers hire through Waterloo co-op [10]. Employers will not prioritize skill retention on their own unless ECE sets the expectation.
Action Plan • Built on Existing Waterloo Systems [7], [8], [9], [11]

Three measures ECE can pilot next term

Zero new bureaucratic systems needed—each recommendation upgrades a process Waterloo already runs.

A
Builds on Course Outlines [7] & ECE 198 [11]

Pilot AI Framework in a 2nd-Year Design Course

Free AI use for repetitive tasks Document & justify AI decisions No-AI debugging checks Sample oral defenses Train conceptual questions [3]
Addresses upper-year students’ call to teach modern AI coding CLIs, AI architecture, and LLM integration while verifying comprehension.
B
Upgrades Mid-Term Eval [8]

Require a 20-Min Midterm Co-op Learning Check-In

Required on WaterlooWorks Week 6 or 7 of work term Checks technical growth & design decisions 1-page employer guide (Appendix B)
Turns Waterloo’s informal mid-term evaluation suggestion [8] into a condition of hiring ECE students.
C
Builds on Rate My Work Term [9]

Track ECE-Specific Co-op Outcomes

Placement rates Role responsibilities Student-reported AI reliance Aggregated Check-In ratings (Rec B)
Isolates Waterloo ECE trends within a year, since national statistics group occupations broadly [1], [2].
Appendix B • One-Page Employer Onboarding Guide

The 4 Questions Supervisors Ask at Week 6–7

A 20-minute conversation checking what the student has learned in addition to what they produced:

QUESTION 01What have you built or changed so far this term?
QUESTION 02Which part of that work could you rebuild or debug without AI tools?
QUESTION 03Explain one design decision you made and why you chose it.
QUESTION 04What skill will you focus on for the rest of the term?
